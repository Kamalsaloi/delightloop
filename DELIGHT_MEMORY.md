# Delight memory

Short product truths. One line each.

## Gifts
- In stock: in our warehouses, ships in 24h–3 days.
- On demand: not in stock; we buy it after it's sent. Label it "On demand".
- Don't hide on-demand gifts by default ("In stock only" starts off).
- Preset = One gift, or Delight choice (recipient picks 1 of up to 3, min 2).
- Everything comes from a preset: gift, gift type, postcard, sticker. A step can't change them.
- Step "How they get it": Preset, or Personal choice (Soon). One gift / Delight choice is set in the preset.
- Need another gift? Edit the preset or switch presets.
- Gifty finds addresses. Never ask the recipient; the creator fills gaps.

## Letters
- 3 templates: Brand envelope, Postcard, Signed postcard. No "V2" in names.
- Signer only for Signed postcard: a workspace member (with photo) or someone else (name + title).
- Postcard has no signer: never show "Signed by" on it.
- Titles: preset list plus "Other" to type one.
- No workspace members → "Add member" takes them to the members page.
- Sticker is optional: a checkbox reveals the stickers. No "No sticker" tile.
- No return address in the letter preset.

## Email
- Picking a person uses all their mailboxes.
- Inactive people can be picked: they send manually from that account, or make it active.
- Senders list groups: Picked, Active, Inactive; each group gets View all when long.
- Senders can be zero: export the emails + details after the play runs, send manually.
- Sending window: workspace window selected by default; Custom sets one per config.

## LinkedIn
- Same senders rules as email (inactive pickable, zero allowed, manual export).
- Actions: Invite, Message, InMail, React, Comment. Follow and Visit not available yet.
- React and Comment wait for a person's approval and need Engagement on in Settings.
- Daily caps live in Settings, Budget: 6 invites, 30 messages, 10 reactions, 3 comments.

## Money
- Everything is paid from the wallet.
- On-demand gifts are charged at launch, before we buy them.
- Wallet too low at launch → blocked, "Top up $X" fix card.
- Letters and gifts are priced separately; show separate lines.
- Presets show per-gift price only; totals live in the play.

## Gift and letter steps · Advanced
- Prod has 3 fields: Reach on, Deadline date, Note. Keep all 3.
- Reach on = "Arrive by": should land by this day.
- Deadline date = "Don't send after": later arrivals skip the step, no charge.
- Note: for the warehouse, every parcel. Recipients never see it.
- For now: empty date = off, past dates disabled. No edge cases.
- Later (parked): split people who can't make the date; letters only get express, ship anyway, skip.

## The loop
- Booking a meeting early exits the loop; no gift.
- Gift reasons cite real signals (LinkedIn, X, behaviour).
- Drafts show only known-upfront numbers (people, addresses, cost).
